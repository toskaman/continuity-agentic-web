# Brag Plan: CONTINUITY  (Brag Full · `--voice` · tone: polished/cinematic)

Brag Full is the creative north star of the v2 jury film. This plan defines the 20-second launch cut (`brag.mp4`) **and** the visual language reused by the 1:51 jury film (hook, palette, type, focus system, lockup). The jury film is the full-length extension required by the competition; the brag cut is its trailer.

## Step 1 rubric (inspection of the real project)
1. **What is the app?** An open agentic website that re-plans one life change across *other companies'* agentic websites, holds replacements first, asks for one bound approval, and has every provider verify a signed receipt on its own page.
2. **Most impressive claim:** "One change. Every commitment re-aligned." — proven live: 3 providers' own pages flip to Friday with "Cross-Origin Ed25519 Receipt Verified", PowerFlow stays untouched.
3. **Visual hook:** the date moving from TUE 17 to FRI 20 and the six bookings turning red; then the four live provider pages flipping green.
4. **UI to show:** the provider wall ("Watch the providers react"), the bound approval dialog, the hero with the coordinator wheel.
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20 s (hook 3 s → reveal 4 s → 2 highlights 9 s → outro 4 s).
6. **Tone:** preset `polished` with `cinematic` reveals; direction: *"quiet premium product film — calm confidence, real UI as the hero."*
7. **Audio:** warm original synth bed (written for this film, no licensed music), sparse UI clicks, three rising verify chimes, one final chime. Narration: Kokoro `af_heart` (brag `--voice`).
8. **User flow worth showing:** request → approval dialog → providers verify receipts.
9. **What makes it specific:** the receipts are verified on the providers' own origins — no other demo can show four independent websites reacting.

## The angle
Everybody has lived the moving-day cascade. The video makes one date change visibly *break* six bookings, then shows four independent websites heal themselves — each one checking a signature on its own page.

## Hook (0–3 s)
TUE 17 flips to FRI 20; six booking cards snap red "still Tuesday". VO: "Move one date, and six bookings break."

- The provider wall: three columns flip to Friday with a green verify flash; PowerFlow stays on Tuesday.

## Outro / punchline
CONTINUITY mark → "One change. Every commitment re-aligned." → `toskaman.github.io/continuity-agentic-web`.

## User flow worth showing
Describe change → approve the bound plan → providers verify their receipts.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed + sparse professional accents
- Music: original bed `music_bed.wav` (synthesized for this production; licence-free by construction)
- Music treatment: soft entry, ducks under the voice, swells into the final chime
- Music cue guidance: no bundled preset (original track); cues are the narration words (word-level ASR timings)
- Audio-reactive treatment: none (restraint)
- SFX posture: sparse — flip tick, card ticks, click on approve, three verify chimes, final chime
- Restraint rule: SFX stay ≥ 8 LU under the voice

## Storyboard
### Scene 1 — Hook — 0–3.0 s
Calendar flip TUE→FRI; six real booking names snap red. Audio: flip tick + low error blip. → hard-soft cut
### Scene 2 — Reveal — 3.0–6.8 s
3D board: five real home pages on five origins stand up, beams draw. VO "Continuity re-plans across four independent provider websites". Audio: soft whoosh, ticks per pane. → blur dissolve
### Scene 3 — Approval — 6.8–10.1 s
Real bound approval dialog, "Operations to commit" outlined; chip "Nothing runs before you approve". → light sweep
### Scene 4 — Providers verify — 10.1–15.6 s
Provider wall: three columns flip to Friday with green flash + chimes; PowerFlow ring "Consulted — nothing to change". Sequential: 3 flips, ~0.5 s apart (accent, readable labels held afterwards).
### Scene 5 — Outro — 15.6–20.0 s
Lockup + URL, final chime, fade.

**Music mood:** calm, premium, resolving. **Audio summary:** a tense pulse resolves into a warm major bed; the verify chimes are the payoff.
